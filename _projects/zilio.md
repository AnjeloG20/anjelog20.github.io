## Understanding the Brand

Before developing the feed, I explored the role ZILIO could play in the online fashion experience.

The brand sits between **fashion and technology**, aiming to improve how customers understand fit, identity, and personal style when shopping online.

## Content Pillars

I developed four recurring content pillars to give the feed a consistent structure while still allowing variety:

**Tech · Style · Fashion · Culture**

  <div class="col-12">

  </div>
</div>

<p class="mt-4">
  These pillars helped define what the brand should talk about — from virtual try-on technology and sizing education to fashion inspiration, runway content, and community-led storytelling.
</p>

## Tone of Voice

The visual identity also needed a consistent writing style.

I explored short captions built around themes of **precision, confidence, individuality, and fit** rather than relying on purely promotional messaging.

## Recommended Rollout

The project concluded with a two-stage content rollout.

Stage one focused on improving the existing profile's appearance, descriptions, and consistency. Stage two expanded the strategy into Reels, a weekly posting calendar, and organised Instagram Stories.
