## Overview

An academic promotional design project developed for **Collarts (Australian College of the Arts)**, focused on presenting the college, its programs, campuses, and industry opportunities through a clear and visually engaging pamphlet.

## Design Concept

The pamphlet uses a bold **black-and-white visual identity** supported by circular imagery and colourful geometric accents. The layout was designed to organise a large amount of institutional information while maintaining a creative look appropriate for an arts-focused college.

The visual hierarchy gives prominence to Collarts' identity while guiding readers through information about the college, industry collaborations, study options, campuses, and programs.

## Information & Layout

The pamphlet presents key information across a structured tri-fold format, including:

- An introduction to Collarts and its educational approach
- Industry collaborations and placement opportunities
- Online and blended study options
- Campus locations
- Programs across entertainment, communication, design, and performing arts
- Contact and social media information

## Project Focus

This project demonstrates experience in:

- Promotional and editorial layout design
- Visual hierarchy and information organisation
- Brand-focused communication
- Typography and image composition
- Designing marketing collateral for a defined audience

*This was an academic project created as part of coursework and was not an official Collarts promotional publication.*
