ZILIO

Instagram Rebranding & Visual Storytelling

During my digital marketing internship at ZILIO Apparel, I developed a visual direction and mock Instagram feed designed to strengthen the brand's social media storytelling.

The project explored ZILIO's audience, brand tone, content pillars, and Instagram strategy before translating those insights into a more cohesive visual identity.

Project Overview

Visual direction developed for ZILIO's Instagram presence.

Understanding the Brand

Before developing the feed, I explored the role ZILIO could play in the online fashion experience.

The brand sits between fashion and technology, aiming to improve how customers understand fit, identity, and personal style when shopping online.

Defining the brand's purpose and the audience the content needed to speak to.

Why Instagram?

Instagram was chosen as the primary platform because it allowed the brand to combine visual storytelling, product education, community content, and lifestyle inspiration.

Content Pillars

I developed four recurring content pillars to give the feed a consistent structure while still allowing variety:

Tech · Style · Fashion · Culture

These pillars helped define what the brand should talk about — from virtual try-on technology and sizing education to fashion inspiration, runway content, and community-led storytelling.

Building the Visual Direction

The brand tone was designed around a contrast between polished and raw, with darker blue tones helping reinforce the technology side of the brand.

Exploring tone, colour, fashion imagery, and the balance between technology and lifestyle.

Tone of Voice

The visual identity also needed a consistent writing style.

I explored short captions built around themes of precision, confidence, individuality, and fit rather than relying on purely promotional messaging.

Translating the Strategy Into a Feed

The next step was applying the four content pillars to an Instagram grid.

Rather than making each post look identical, the goal was to create visual rhythm while allowing different themes to appear throughout the feed.

Mock feed showing how Tech, Fashion, Style, and Culture could cycle throughout the Instagram grid.

Reel Concepts

The visual system was also extended into short-form video.

Each Reel concept was designed around the same four pillars, creating opportunities for educational, fashion-led, styling, and cultural content.

Audience Personas

To make the content direction more specific, I developed audience personas representing different types of customers who could connect with ZILIO.

Three audience profiles exploring differences in lifestyle, values, Instagram behaviour, motivations, and pain points.

Recommended Rollout

The project concluded with a two-stage content rollout.

Stage one focused on improving the existing profile’s appearance, descriptions, and consistency. Stage two expanded the strategy into Reels, a weekly posting calendar, and organised Instagram Stories.

Project Takeaways

This project allowed me to work across both strategy and visual execution — from understanding ZILIO’s audience and brand positioning to developing content pillars, tone, Instagram concepts, audience personas, and a proposed publishing structure.
